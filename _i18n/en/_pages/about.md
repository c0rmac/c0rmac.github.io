I am a PhD candidate in mathematics at the Technical University of Munich, supervised by Prof. Massimo Fornasier at the Chair of Applied Numerical Analysis, and a member of the <a href="https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/aipd/members/cormac-kinsella/">ATARI Lab</a>, led by Prof. Majid Khadiv, at the Munich Institute of Robotics and Machine Intelligence. I hold a BSc in Mathematics from Trinity College Dublin (2023) and an MSc in Mathematics and Informatics from TUM (2026).

My research is on consensus-based optimisation (CBO) on manifolds: a swarm of interacting particles works together to find the global minimum of a non-convex function on a curved space. In robotics, I apply these methods to high-dimensional problems, in particular diverse scene generation. Given a scene, the goal is to find one example of every distinct way its objects can come to rest.

Before my PhD I worked as a software engineer, most recently at the BMW Group, where I built numerical libraries and data hubs. I am also an Irish speaker of the Cois Fharraige dialect of Connemara, and helped translate apps and games into Irish, including the official Irish translation of ***Among Us*** ([covered by The Verge](https://web.archive.org/web/20210718192147/https://www.theverge.com/2021/7/16/22579968/among-us-official-irish-translation)).
